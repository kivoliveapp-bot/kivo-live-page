Kivo Live Child Safety Standards

Standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Kivo Live is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all users.

Kivo Live has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, or any other form of abuse involving children.

Any activity that violates applicable child-safety laws or these standards may result in immediate action, including removal of content, suspension or termination of accounts, and reporting to appropriate authorities where required by law.

2. Prohibited Content and Behavior

Kivo Live does not allow users to create, upload, share, request, distribute, promote, or facilitate:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation of children.
  • Sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to sexually exploit minors.
  • Requests for sexual images or videos involving children.
  • Sexual solicitation of minors.
  • Trafficking or exploitation of children.
  • Content that encourages or facilitates child abuse.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Kivo Live provides users with in-app tools to report users and content that may violate our safety standards.

Users should report suspected child sexual exploitation, inappropriate interactions involving minors, or suspected CSAM immediately through the available reporting features in the app.

Reports are reviewed and appropriate action may be taken based on the circumstances and applicable laws.

4. Action Against Violations

When Kivo Live identifies or receives a report concerning prohibited child-safety activity, we may take appropriate enforcement action, including:

  • Removing or restricting violating content.
  • Suspending user accounts.
  • Terminating user accounts.
  • Blocking users from using Kivo Live.
  • Preserving relevant information when legally required.
  • Reporting suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

5. Cooperation With Authorities

Kivo Live will comply with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. Where required by law, we may cooperate with appropriate regional or national authorities and provide relevant information in response to valid legal requests.

We may also report suspected child sexual exploitation or other illegal activity to the appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

6. User Blocking and Safety Tools

Kivo Live provides safety features that allow users to block other users and report inappropriate behavior.

Users are encouraged to use these tools whenever they encounter behavior that violates Kivo Live's rules or creates a safety concern.

7. Prevention and Enforcement

Kivo Live maintains safety standards designed to prevent the use of its services for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

We may review reports and other available information to identify violations and take appropriate enforcement action consistent with our policies and applicable law.

8. Child Safety Contact

For child-safety concerns, including suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child sexual exploitation, contact:

Email: kivoliveapp@gmail.com

This contact is intended for child-safety and CSAM-related concerns. Please provide sufficient information to help us understand and investigate the report.

9. Compliance With Applicable Laws

Kivo Live is committed to complying with applicable child-safety, online-safety, and reporting requirements in the jurisdictions where the service operates.

These standards may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services, applicable laws, and safety requirements.

10. Updates to These Standards

Kivo Live may update these Child Safety Standards when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.
